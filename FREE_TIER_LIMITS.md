# Free tier limits

Provider documentation checked November 20, 2026. Verify the project and organization plans in the service dashboards before deployment since limits can change.

| Service | Free configuration and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Render | Free Python web service on a Hobby workspace. 512 MB RAM, 0.1 CPU, 750 free instance-hours per workspace per calendar month, and sleep after 15 idle minutes. Restarts can erase local files. TLS is included. Leave payment details unset so bandwidth and build overruns suspend services or stop new builds. Free Render Postgres expires after 30 days, so use Neon for persistence. [Free services](https://render.com/docs/free) · [Compute plans](https://render.com/docs/compute-plans) |
| GroqCloud | Keep the organization on Free. The published Free limits for `openai/gpt-oss-120b` are 30 requests/minute, 1,000 requests/day, 8,000 tokens/minute and 200,000 tokens/day, subject to account limits. A clean job creation uses one request and each resume uses two before retries. Over-limit calls return 429. A payment method is required to upgrade to Developer. [Limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits) · [Billing](https://console.groq.com/docs/billing-faqs) · [Structured output models](https://console.groq.com/docs/structured-outputs) |
| Neon | Free requires no payment details. Current plan docs list 1 GB per project (20 GB account total), 100 CU-hours/project/month and 5 GB of public network transfer per project. Idle Free compute scales to zero after five minutes and wakes on connection. Neon advertises up to 10,000 pooled client connections, with underlying direct connections dependent on compute size. Compute or egress exhaustion suspends the database until the next billing period. Storage exhaustion blocks writes. The consulted plan docs specify no fixed project expiry comparable to Render Free Postgres. [Plans](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans) · [Card-free signup](https://neon.com/variable-load) · [Scale to zero](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/scale-to-zero) · [Connection pooling](https://neon.com/docs/connect/connection-pooling) |
| Streamlit Community Cloud | Free account with GitHub and sign-in, one private app, and invite-only viewing. Resource limits can throttle or stop the app. The published approximate limits are 0.078–2 CPU cores and 690 MB–2.7 GB memory. Apps hibernate after 12 hours without traffic. [Community Cloud](https://docs.streamlit.io/deploy/streamlit-community-cloud) · [App limits](https://docs.streamlit.io/deploy/streamlit-community-cloud/manage-your-app) · [Private sharing](https://docs.streamlit.io/deploy/streamlit-community-cloud/share-your-app) |

Use free plans without enabling paid upgrades, paid add-ons, or payment methods. If a provider requires billing details for your account or region, pause for approval before deploying. Free quotas are shared across users and may interrupt a demo. Local Ollama and SQLite remain available independently of cloud quotas.
